## 🧭 About Me

I'm a final-year Computer Science student at **KFUPM** (AI & Machine Learning concentration) and an undergraduate researcher in **computer vision and multimodal AI**. One question ties my research together:

> 🔍 **How can we make visual AI report only what it can actually see?**

KFUPM selected me for its **Emerging Professor Program**, which prepares students to return as faculty. I'm applying to PhD programs in Computer Science, fully funded by KFUPM, to work on computer vision and multimodal learning.

- 🔬 **Research track**: vision-language models, X-ray instance segmentation, and Arabic NLP, with evaluation that is measurable and reproducible
- 🛠️ **Engineering track**: full-stack products with clean structure, reliable APIs, and thoughtful UX

> 💡 I care about work that can be **justified with evidence**, **shipped with clarity**, and **maintained over time**.

📍 Dhahran, Saudi Arabia &nbsp;·&nbsp; 🎓 KFUPM, B.Sc. expected June 2027

## 🔬 Research

- **Visual hallucinations in vision-language models**: severity-aware preference alignment that cut Object HalBench CHAIRs from 53.0 to 36.0 for LLaVA-1.5-7B. Supervised by Dr. Muzammil Behzad. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20478) · [Code](https://github.com/BRAIN-Lab-AI/Fine-Grained-Hallucination-Detection-and-Severity-Aware-Mitigation-in-Vision-Language-Models)
- **Instance segmentation of overlapping objects in X-ray baggage scans**: a shared point-based mask refiner reaching 72.47 mask AP on PIXray. Supervised by Dr. Qasim Umer. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20476) · [Code](https://github.com/pr-Abdulaziz/ProposalFirst-XRaySeg)
- **Detecting AI-generated Arabic text on social media**: controlled preprocessing and cross-platform evaluation on Twitter/X and YouTube. Supervised by Prof. Tarek Helmy. [Article](https://www.journalssystem.com/juri/Identifying-Arabic-AI-Generated-Text-on-Social-Media-Preprocessing-nChoices-and-Cross,231577,0,2.html) · [Code](https://github.com/pr-Abdulaziz/Arabic-deepfake-detection)
